# Runner licence: FSL-1.1-ALv2

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Source:** Decided by Willem, 2026-11-16, before making the GitHub repository public; proposed by Claude.

## Decision

- The runner is licensed under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License (FSL-1.1-ALv2), copyright Rodo Software. `LICENSE` holds the standard text; `NOTICE`, `README.md` and the OpenAPI file's `info.license` name it.
- Anyone may use, change and share the runner for any purpose except a Competing Use: offering it, or something with the same functionality, in a commercial product or service. Companies running it for their own work, Cinderline's users among them, are allowed.
- Each version becomes available under the Apache License 2.0 two years after its release.
- The README calls the runner "source available", not "open source".
- The repository's history starts with one commit under this licence; the earlier history and releases were removed before the repository was made public.

## Reason

- Willem does not want others with commercial interests to profit from the runner or its code; a permissive licence allows that.
- A plain non-commercial licence would also forbid businesses that use Cinderline from running the runner. FSL forbids only competing products and services.
